# T5

T5 used as a non-diffusion baseline in parts of the SAE analysis (mainly comparison of decoder activations across model families). Not a focus of current work.

## Configs

- `configs/train_t5_sae.yaml`
- `configs/collect_t5_activations.yaml` (train)
- `configs/collect_t5_activations_val.yaml` (validation)
- `configs/interpret_features_t5_layer{00,11,23}.yaml`
- `configs/find_top_examples_t5.yaml`

## Layers analyzed

`[0, 11, 23]` — early, middle, late of a 24-layer decoder. Same 3-layer sampling logic as Plaid.

## Notes

- Single "timestep" since T5 is not a diffusion model. The activation collection pipeline still writes `timestep_XXXX.pt` files but with just one timestep.
- Not used in recent Plaid fine-tuning work. Mentioned here for cross-reference when comparing feature types across model families.
